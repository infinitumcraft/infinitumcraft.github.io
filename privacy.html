Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

Kitsu collects only what's needed to run its features. Your data is never sold or shared with third parties.

01

What We Collect

The following data is stored in our database to power Kitsu's features:

DataWhy it's stored
User IDEconomy balance, XP/level, daily rewards, birthday, roleplay counters
Guild (Server) IDServer settings, welcome/farewell config, moderation configuration
Birthday dateOptional — only if you set it with a birthday command
Moderator & warning recordsWarnings issued include the moderator's User ID, target User ID, and reason
Giveaway dataGuild ID, channel ID, message ID, host User ID, prize, end time
Starboard dataMessage IDs and star counts for pinned messages
Mute recordsUser ID, Guild ID, and expiry time for timed mutes
Anime watchlistPer-server anime tracking — anime title, ID, last episode

02

What We Don't Collect

  • Message content (we never read or store your messages)
  • Email addresses or any personal information beyond Discord IDs
  • Payment or financial data of any kind
  • Data from servers Kitsu is not added to

03

How We Use Your Data

All collected data is used exclusively to operate Kitsu's features — economy, levelling, moderation, giveaways, birthdays, and so on. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics sold to third parties, or any purpose beyond running the bot.

04

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any third party. Data may only be disclosed if required by law.

05

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as you or your server uses Kitsu. If Kitsu is removed from a server, that server's configuration data can be deleted upon request. Users may also request deletion of their personal data at any time.

06

Your Rights

You may request access to or deletion of any data linked to your User ID by contacting us via the support server. We will process your request as soon as possible.

07

Third-Party Services

Kitsu operates on Discord's platform and is subject to Discord's Privacy Policy. We have no control over data collected directly by Discord.

08

Changes

This policy may be updated at any time. Significant changes will be announced in the support server.

To request data deletion or ask a privacy question, contact us via the infi@kitsubuisness.fr.
